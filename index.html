About Kane PC

We've been keeping businesses online longer than most of the apps running on them.

Kane PC, Inc. has been keeping businesses online since 2001 — what started as a one-person technology shop in Westchester County has grown into a boutique managed services firm based in White Plains, New York. Today we support medical practices, law firms, accounting firms, contractors, restaurants, nonprofits, and more — organizations where a downed server or a broken camera feed isn't an inconvenience, it's a revenue event.

Our sweet spot is the business that's outgrown the "my nephew handles IT" era but doesn't want to be a ticket in someone else's queue. You'll know the people who work on your systems. They'll know your network, your people, and what normal looks like at your office. That's what boutique means to us.

We partner with best-in-class platforms — NinjaOne for monitoring, CrowdStrike Falcon Complete for endpoint security, Microsoft 365 and Azure AD, Ubiquiti for networking, Control4 for automation — and we build software in-house when an off-the-shelf answer doesn't fit.
